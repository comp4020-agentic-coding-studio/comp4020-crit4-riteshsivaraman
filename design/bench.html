The direction §2.1

Risograph overprint. Three spot inks printed on warm paper in three passes that don't quite line up.

Chosen over the obvious dark-canvas-with-glow because glow is where every generative audio toy lands, and because overprint does conceptual work no other direction does: where two inks overlap they genuinely mix, so colour mixing is genetic mixing. The metaphor isn't applied to the visuals — it is the same mechanism.

Ink §2.2

Three inks multiply into six perceivable colours. A constrained palette that behaves like a rich one — the whole argument for the medium.

Ink is derived, never stored §5.3 · invariant I7

Move a gene and the colour moves with it. Nothing stores a hue, so a child differs in ink by exactly as much as it differs in sound — that is what makes evolution visible.

The signature: the press slips §2.5

The layers sit at a fixed registration offset. On a collision the offset jolts — proportional to energy, settling over JOLT_DECAY_MS. A loud note looks like the press slipping.

0.70

Base offsets in CSS pixels — pink (+1.2, −0.4), blue (−0.9, +0.8), yellow (+0.3, +1.1); a jolt scales them by 1 + energy × 3.5.
